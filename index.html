Designed and built gentoi.online from scratch as a custom-coded web gateway for my Garry's Mod community. The site handles Steam OpenID authentication, a transactional package store tied directly to in-game entitlements, a public ban registry with searchable history, and a full user database recording sign-ups, sessions, and key gameplay metrics.

The store has processed over 2,000 real purchases, and the site routinely brings in thousands of monthly visitors who funnel through Steam auth into the game server. Every transaction, ban, and player record syncs back to the production MySQL backend, making the website the connective tissue between the public-facing community and the live game.
